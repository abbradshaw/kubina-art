A vibrant, multi-layered composition featuring a silhouette procession of white figures across a desert-like landscape. Above them, large white cloud forms float in a dark blue sky. The scene is populated by colorful, abstract bird-like creatures and a row of cryptic white symbols along the bottom, creating a narrative of movement and ritual.
