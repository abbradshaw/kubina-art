A study of nesting forms painted on a wood panel, where a large, wood-toned head contains a smaller, blue humanoid figure within its cheek or jaw area. The heavy, sculpted quality of the large head contrasts with the fragile, almost embryonic nature of the smaller figure, suggesting themes of memory, inner self, or creation.
