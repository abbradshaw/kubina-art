A surreal, nocturnal scene featuring two intertwined figures—one with a white hood/hair, the other with a golden face—strolling against a deep black background dotted with yellow stars. The figures are elongated and fluid, merging into one another, while a decorative blue patterned shape anchors the right side of the composition.
