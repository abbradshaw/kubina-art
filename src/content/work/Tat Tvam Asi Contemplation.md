A intense, psychedelic Mandala-like composition featuring concentric rings of vibrant red, yellow, and white radiating from a central white core. The textured, seemingly organic edges of the rings contrast with the geometric perfection of the circle, creating a hypnotic visual anchor meant for contemplation.
