A playful, narrative composition resembling a theatrical set or a cave painting. A large white shape dominates the center, flanked by dark, tree-like silhouettes. Below, a row of red stick figures dance across a yellow foreground, enacting a scene of celebration or ritual under the "stage" of the main forms.
