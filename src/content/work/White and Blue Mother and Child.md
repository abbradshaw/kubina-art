A minimalist, line-drawn figure of a woman (possibly a mother figure) in white with blue outlines, set against a patterned green background. A smaller blue organic form floats to her right. The figure is seated on a blue grid floor, creating a sense of perspective in an otherwise flat, illustrative space.
