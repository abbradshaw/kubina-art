A vibrant, neo-expressionist portrait rendered in intense primary colors—yellow, red, and blue. The face is segmented into geometric planes with thick black outlines, reminiscent of primitive masks or early modernist experiments. The eyes are particularly striking, with mismatched pupils that add a sense of unease or intensity.
